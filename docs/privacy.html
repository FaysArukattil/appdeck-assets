1. Core Privacy Philosophy: 100% On-Device Processing

AppDeck Studio is engineered on the foundational principle of privacy by design. We believe your creative work, confidential app designs, and unreleased product screenshots belong strictly to you.

Zero Cloud Upload Guarantee

AppDeck Studio performs all 3D mesh rendering, perspective warping, gradient generation, and 4K image rasterization entirely on your local device CPU and GPU. We do not operate remote processing servers, and your images never leave your hardware.

1.1 What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT upload, transmit, monitor, or store your photos, screenshots, or exported designs.
  • We do NOT collect personal identifying information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, or phone number.
  • We do NOT embed third-party analytics trackers, behavioral profiling scripts, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do NOT monetize user data, sell telemetry to brokers, or profile your creative activities.

2. Device Media & Android Photo Picker

AppDeck Studio requires access to your selected images solely to allow you to insert screenshots into device mockup frames.

2.1 Android System Photo Picker

AppDeck Studio integrates the modern Android System Photo Picker. When you tap a device slot to add a screenshot:

  • You select specific images through Android's secure OS-level dialog.
  • AppDeck receives access only to the specific files you explicitly select.
  • AppDeck does not request or possess broad access to your entire photo library or personal document folders.

2.2 Temporary Memory Usage

Imported screenshots are decoded into local volatile memory (RAM) solely for the active editing session and canvas rendering. They are never transmitted over the network or saved to external servers.

3. Local Storage & Sandboxed Files

All project preferences (such as selected device frames, custom gradient parameters, typography font choices, and favorited templates) are stored exclusively within your Android device's private, encrypted, sandboxed storage via Android Keystore and SecureStorage.

3.1 Exported PNG Files

When you trigger a 4K PNG export, the rendered image is saved directly to your local device Pictures directory (e.g. Pictures/AppDeck) via standard MediaStore APIs so you can access it in your gallery. AppDeck does not keep remote copies.

3.2 Network Communication

Network connectivity is utilized strictly for:

  • Google Play In-App Updates to check for verified software updates and security patches.
  • Initial downloading of curated mockup templates from our open-source release assets.

4. Children's Privacy & Contact

AppDeck Studio is a universal utility tool for developers and designers. Because we collect zero personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect, request, or harvest any personal information from children under the age of 13.
